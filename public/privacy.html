Privacy Policy

Pea, by AIfy Solutions · Effective October 7, 2026

The short version

  • Pea has no accounts. Your lists live on your phone, not on our servers.
  • When you speak or type a capture, the recording or text may be sent to be transcribed and sorted, and is then discarded by us.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Pea is made by AIfy Solutions, based in Surrey, British Columbia, Canada. You can reach us through aify.solutions.

What stays on your phone

Your first name, your morning-brief time, and every item on your lists (including any drafted messages) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up to our servers. Choosing Reset Pea in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

What leaves your phone, and why

WhatWhenWhere it goes
Voice recording (up to 30 seconds) When you hold the mic to capture Our server passes it to OpenAI to be transcribed into text.
Text of a capture When Pea can't sort a capture on the phone, or you ask for a drafted message Our server passes it to Anthropic to be sorted or to write the draft.
A random install ID and your IP address With each of the requests above Our server, to apply fair-use limits and prevent abuse.

Your first name is not sent with these requests. Many captures are sorted entirely on your phone and never leave it.

What we keep

Our server does not store your recordings or the text of your captures; it relays them and returns the result to your phone. Usage counters tied to the install ID and IP address are kept for up to 24 hours. Our hosting provider keeps standard short-lived request logs (such as time, IP address and response status) for operating and securing the service.

Companies that process data for us

  • OpenAI — speech-to-text transcription. Privacy policy
  • Anthropic — sorting captures and writing drafts. Privacy policy
  • Vercel — hosting our server. Privacy policy
  • Upstash — storing the short-lived usage counters, where enabled. Privacy policy

These companies handle the data under their own terms and may retain it for a limited period for purposes such as abuse monitoring. They may process data outside Canada, including in the United States. Please avoid including information in a capture that you would not want processed this way.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, or messages. When you send a draft, Pea hands the text to your phone's Messages app; you choose the recipient and send it yourself.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you hold the mic button, to record a voice capture.
  • Notifications — to deliver your morning brief. These are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.

You can turn either off in your phone's settings; typing a capture still works without the microphone.

Children

Pea is designed for parents and other adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because your lists are stored only on your device and we hold no account for you, you control and can delete that data yourself at any time. If you have a question about the limited information described above, or a request under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) or another law that applies to you, contact us through aify.solutions.

Changes

If we change how Pea handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
